Subscription Model Assumptions

Foundation fee: $40,000 one-time platform setup (billed at contract signing), not included in monthly figures.

Renewal: 20% reduction across all agents effective Month 25 (Sep 2028) after 24-month initial term.

Fully managed: Subscription includes all infrastructure, monitoring, optimization, model upgrades, and operational support. No AI staffing or infrastructure management required by ORL.

Early termination: 18 Month Lock-In Required. 60-day written notice required after 18 months and Penalty of 50% of the then-current monthly subscription rate for each remaining month on the contract up to 24 months.

One-Time Model Assumptions (All Ongoing Costs Are Estimates)

Cost structure: Build and maintenance are billed by Insight. Infrastructure and token costs are estimated client-direct expenses paid by ORL to cloud and API providers.

Build payment: Agent build costs ($463,000 total) billed at phase delivery milestones. No separate platform or foundation fee.

Estimated maintenance: First 2 months FREE. Starting M3, ~$600/mo per live agent, scaling with deployment — $1,800 (M3) to $4,800/mo at full deployment (M9+). Covers monitoring, patching, model updates, incident response, and ongoing provider enhancements. All maintenance figures are estimates and may vary based on actual usage and complexity.

Estimated enhancements: All enhancement figures will be estimated upon submission of change requests and may vary based on actual usage and complexity.

Estimated client infrastructure (paid by ORL): Azure compute, APIM, Twilio, ElevenLabs, identity verification APIs. Estimated to ramp from ~$320/mo (M1) to ~$3,040/mo (Y2+) as agents deploy and call volume grows. Based on 5-layer architecture estimate sized for ORL's current volume of ~66,750 total calls/mo (~6,400 CES calls/mo) and associated document throughput. Actual costs may vary based on call volume, document throughput, and vendor pricing changes.

Estimated token costs (paid by ORL): ~$985/mo estimated at full deployment for Claude API inference. Modeled on ORL's current call volume (~6,400 CES calls/mo, ~66,750 total calls/mo) and document processing load across all 8 agents. Actual token usage will depend on transaction volume, query complexity, and model selection.

Code ownership: ORL owns the codebase. Insight provides maintenance as a service, not managed ops.
